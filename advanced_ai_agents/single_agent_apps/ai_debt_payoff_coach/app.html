              Where your data lives

              Your numbers are saved in this browser on this device.

              • The app never asks for bank logins, card numbers or your SSN. You enter only amounts, dates and store names.
              • CSV files are read on your device. Choosing a file uploads nothing.
              • Your numbers go to an AI only when you send a message in the Coach tab.
